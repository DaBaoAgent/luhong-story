01 The story

The boss rides the product.

Lu Hong was born in Suzhou in 1979. Cerebral palsy affects his speech and his coordination. He built a photo-album workshop into a business that employs people with disabilities, and was named a Touching China figure for 2022.

In 2025 he started XinZhi Power with a simple question: why should a good manual wheelchair need a second chair to get outside?

The first prototype was cut from 16 kg to 12 kg. The lightest production model is now 10 kg. Lu Hong is still the first test rider — strapped in, measuring steep slopes, waiting in the rain for the electronics to prove themselves.

“I used to ask why people laughed at me. Now I ask myself why I can't do better.”

Lu Hong, Touching China 2022

The people who use these chairs every day are the people who design, test and build them. That is not a slogan here. It is the quality-control process.

03 Fit

Fits the chair you already own.

The add-on replaces nothing. It clamps to the front of a manual wheelchair and takes the weight off the front casters; you steer with its handlebars. Your chair still folds, still fits in the car, and is still your chair when the unit is off.

Mount options vary by model: clamp-on, first-generation center link, folding-wheelchair link, stainless-steel link and second-generation quick-release. Send us your wheelchair model and a photo of the frame — we will confirm the link and the center-of-gravity setting before shipping.

04 The workshop

Built in Suzhou, tested on real roads.

XinZhi Power was founded in July 2025 and sold more than 3,000 units in its first year, including a single 1,300-unit export order. New models are road-tested by wheelchair users before release.

Who builds it

64 people. Nearly 70% live with a disability. The workshop grew out of Lu Hong's album business, which has employed people with disabilities since the early 2000s.

How it is tested

Prototypes are ridden by wheelchair users on real roads. Employees have taken early units on 50 km-plus trips around Lake Tai. A spec sheet is the start of the test, not the end.

What we ship

Complete units, spare batteries, chargers and mounting links. Tell us your market and importer requirements; we confirm the documentation available for the model before you order.
